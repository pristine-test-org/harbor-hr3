Prepare for a consult

A 45-minute consult goes furthest when the advisor knows the situation up front, so take ten minutes to gather the details below before you book.

  • Who is involved, and what their roles are.
  • What has happened so far, with dates.
  • Any policy or handbook section that applies.
  • The decision you need to make this week.
